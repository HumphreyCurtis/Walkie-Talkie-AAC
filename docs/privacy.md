# Privacy Policy — Walkie Talkie AAC

_Last updated: 2 August 2026_

Walkie Talkie AAC does not collect, store or transmit any personal information.

## No account, no server

There is no sign-up, no login and no backend. The app has no analytics, no
advertising, no third-party SDKs and no in-app purchases. Nothing you write is
sent anywhere.

## What is stored, and where

Your badges are saved in a file called `Badges.json` inside the app's own
Documents directory on your device. Your settings — voice, speed, and how
badges face — are stored in the app's own preferences.

Both are removed when you delete the app. They are included in an encrypted
device backup only if you have one turned on, which is between you and Apple.

## Microphone and speech recognition

Symbol Speak uses the microphone and Apple's speech recognition to show a
picture for the word being said. Recognition runs on your device where your
device supports it. Nothing is recorded, saved or uploaded by this app — the
transcript exists only while the screen is open and is discarded when you leave
it.

Apple's own handling of speech recognition is covered by
[Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Camera and photos

Photo to Speech classifies a photo on your device using a bundled model, to
suggest a word. Photos are not saved by this app and are never uploaded.

## Links and the AI import

Search and the links on the About and Aphasia pages open Safari. Once you leave
the app, those websites' own privacy policies apply.

The "Write badges with AI" feature works entirely through your clipboard. The
app copies a prompt for you and reads what you paste back. It holds no API key
and contacts no AI service. You choose which assistant to paste into, and that
assistant's own privacy policy applies to what you send it. You can read the
prompt before you send it.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

## Changes

Any change to this policy will be published in this file in the app's public
repository.

## Contact

Questions or concerns: open an issue at
<https://github.com/HumphreyCurtis/Walkie-Talkie-AAC/issues>. Please do not
